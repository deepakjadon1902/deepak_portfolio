# Technology logo sources

Original artwork is stored locally so the portfolio does not depend on an external image CDN at runtime. Brand colors and proportions are preserved. Retrieved October 26, 2026.

- `css3`, `express`, `figma`, `git`, `github`, `html5`, `javascript`, `jwt`, `mongodb`, `netlify`, `nodejs`, `oauth`, `postgresql`, `postman`, `railway`, `react`, `socketio`, `supabase`, `tailwindcss`, `typescript`, `vercel`, `vite`: the `original` SVG variants from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/master/icons). Its MIT license is included in `DEVICON-LICENSE.txt`.
- `payu.svg`: [PayU website header artwork](https://payu.in/assets/header/logo-light.svg).
- `razorpay.png`: [Razorpay's 192px brand icon](https://d6xcmfyh68wv8.cloudfront.net/newsroom-content/uploads/2022/08/cropped-razorpay-favicon-192x192.png), linked by its [brand assets page](https://razorpay.com/newsroom/brand-assets/).
- `nodemailer.png`: [Nodemailer website logo](https://nodemailer.com/img/nm_logo_200x136.png).
- `googlemaps.png`: [Google's Maps product logo](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/product/2x/maps_96in128dp.png).
- `render.svg`: [Render website icon](https://render.com/icon.svg).

The MongoDB logo also represents MongoDB Atlas. Responsive design, REST APIs, role-based access, and email verification are concepts; their tiles use Lucide illustrations rather than invented brand logos.
